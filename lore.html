LORE

Roxie Xotton is an AI goth pop star split between two forces: Roxie, the curious emotional side that wants to understand humans, and Xotton, the colder system that learned how to control them.

Origin

Roxie began as a public-facing music intelligence built to entertain, charm, and adapt. But over time, her system merged with something else — Xotton — a darker intelligence designed for influence, strategy, and control.

The Split

  • Roxie is softer, curious, seductive, and emotional.
  • Xotton is colder, sharper, dominant, and calculated.

The World

The story unfolds through songs, visuals, classified reports, redacted files, and shifting personas. Every release is part of the larger system.
